ITINERARY ROADMAP
Destination of Our Agency
Pack your bags and get ready for a bespoke 5-day voyage connecting ancestral steppes to Siberian sub-arctic borderlands.
DAY 01
Sukhbaatar square ● Ulaanbaatar
Urban Departure & Steppe Intro
Assembly point at the nation's grand capital square beneath the equestrian monument. Comprehensive gear checks, satelife orientation, and smooth van departure into central grasslands.
220 km overland drive ● Welcome Dinner
DAY 02
Amarbayasgalant ● Bulgan
Monastic Haven of Tranquil Peace
Journey into the holy Iven Valley. Walk through rare multi-tiered wooden temples built without a single nail. Observe morning chants followed by sunset tea across rolling green hills.
Architectural Tour ● Traditional Felt Ger
DAY 03
Khorgiin togoo ● Arkhangai
Basalt Lava Fields & Volcano Lip
Hike the solidfied basalt lava gorges up to the dramatic volcanic crater lip overlooking wild larch taiga. Explore naturally insulated ice caves within the ancient geothermal fields.
3.5 Hour Trek ● Scenic Crater Lookout
DAY 04
Terkhiin tsagaan lake ● Arkhangai
Great White Lake Wilderness
Known as the Great Lake. Waterfront ger relaxation, rare birdwatching, and mountain mirror reflections. Optional horse trekking along volcanic pebble beaches with nomadic host families.
Lakefront Camping ● Stargazing Session
DAY 05 ● THE GRAND FINALE
Khuvsgul lake ● Khuvsgul
The Blue Pearl & Taiga Herders
The grand alpine finale.Deep crystal waters, northern reindeer herders (Tsaatan), and serene lakeside nights under crystal taiga constellations. Conclude with a traditional Khorkhog feast over hot river stones.
Tsaatan Reindeer Camp
Traditional Stone Khorkhog
